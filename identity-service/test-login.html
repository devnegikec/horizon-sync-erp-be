🍪 Remember Me Feature Test

Test cookie-based authentication with persistent sessions

Login

How It Works

🔒 Without Remember Me

Session cookie expires when browser closes. Perfect for shared computers.

✅ With Remember Me

Cookie persists for 30 days. Stay logged in even after closing browser.

🍪 Cookie Settings

Development: HTTP allowed, SameSite=lax
Production: HTTPS required, SameSite=none

🔐 Security

Cookies are HTTP-only (no JavaScript access) and include CSRF protection.

📝 Testing Steps

1. Login without Remember Me
2. Close browser and reopen
3. Session should be expired
4. Login with Remember Me
5. Close browser and reopen
6. Session should persist
